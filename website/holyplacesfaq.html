Holy Places of the Lord

Frequently Asked Questions

Recording visits

How do I add a new visit?

Open the place from the Places tab, then tap Record Visit on the Place Details page.

After you have at least one visit for a place, swipe left on that visit for shortcuts:

  • Copy creates a new visit with the same details and moves the date forward by the number of days set in Settings.
  • New creates a visit for the same place with today’s date.

On iOS you can also tap the Add button on the Visits tab. Choose Other there to record a visit to a custom holy place that is not in the list.

How do I record a visit from the past?

When recording a visit, tap the date to change it. It defaults to today, so change it for past visits. To correct a visit you already entered, open it from the Visits tab and tap Edit.

How do I delete a visit?

On the Visits tab, swipe left on a visit and tap Delete. Deleting the app and reinstalling it will also remove all visits on that device, so export a backup first if you still want the data.

How do I track hours as a temple ordinance worker?

In Settings (from the Home tab), turn on Enable Hours Worked Entry. That adds an hours field on the Record Visit page and an hours-worked line on the Summary tab.

Backup and devices

How do I back up my visits? Where is the data stored?

Visit data is stored in the app’s database on your device. On iOS it can also be included in nightly iCloud device backups if those are enabled. On Android, backup behavior depends on your device and Google account settings. Restoring a new device from the same backup should restore your visits. If the app is deleted, or you set up a new device without restoring from backup, the visits will be lost.

The most reliable protection is the app’s own export. On the Visits tab, tap Export (top right) and save an XML file to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or your device. You can optionally include visit photos in the XML export. To restore, choose Import Visits from XML and select that file.

You can also export a text or CSV copy of your visits from the same screen. Those formats are useful for reading or spreadsheets, but XML is the format used to restore visits into the app.

Can I sync visits between devices?

Visits are stored locally and do not sync automatically. Export them as XML on one device and import that file on the other. Matching place and date are treated as duplicates and skipped, even if the comments differ. Export regularly as a backup in case the app is deleted.

Profiles

How do I track visits for family members separately?

Turn on Enable Profiles in Settings, then use Manage Profiles to add family members (up to 10). Switch the active profile from the Home tab. Goals, summary totals, achievements, and exports follow the active profile.

When recording a visit, you can assign it to more than one profile at once. If it is not only for the active profile, a “Visit Recorded for:” line is added to the notes.

How do I copy visits to another profile?

With Profiles enabled and at least two profiles, tap the Select button on the Visits tab. Search or filter if you want a subset, select the visits (or Select All), then tap Copy to Profile…. This copies the visits; it does not move or delete them from the current profile.

Map and more

How do I see only the places I have visited on the map?

On the Map tab, tap Filters and choose the Visited option. You can also filter by place type, such as active temples or historical sites.

What is Map Timeline?

Tap Timeline on the Map tab to watch temples appear across the world by dedicated year, from Kirtland (1836) and the original Nauvoo Temple (1846) through today. Tap Play for an animated journey. Pins show the name in use at that time.

How do visit reminders work?

On iOS, Settings includes Enable Visit Notifications under Track Visit Reminder. After you spend time at a holy place, the app can remind you to record the visit. You must allow Location (Always) and Notifications. You can also set how many minutes to wait after the visit before the reminder appears, and optionally limit reminders to certain place types.

Does the app have widgets or an Apple Watch app?

Yes, on iOS:

  • Large widget: a featured visit photo (or place image), with the place name and date. Tapping it opens that visit.
  • Medium widget: your latest achievement and current-year goal progress.
  • Small widget: a daily inspirational quote.

The Apple Watch app is a temple-session timer. Swipe down to adjust the minutes, swipe left or right to change the background image, and tap to reset the countdown.

What are Achievements and the Celebration Board?

Achievements track milestones such as temples visited or ordinances completed. Open them from the Home tab. Completed achievements can be shared as an image.

Higher-tier achievements can also be posted to the public Celebration Board, where others can see the milestone and the places included.

How do I share the app with someone?

On the Home tab, tap Share to send an App Store or Google Play link, show a QR code, or open a printable promotional PDF.

Is there an Android version?

Yes. Holy Places of the Lord is on Google Play as well as the App Store. Widgets and the Apple Watch app are currently iOS-only.
