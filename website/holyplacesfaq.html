Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I add a new visit?

A: To add a new visit, navigate to the desired place from the Places tab to the Place Details page for the place you want to create a visit. From there you will see the “Record Visit“ button. Once you have added a visit for a place, you can also swipe left on that visit to reveal shortcut options of Copy or New: Select ‘Copy’ to record a new visit with same details and it will automatically move out date of new visit a configurable number of days based on option in Settings; Select ‘New’ to quickly create new visit for same place with today's date. An alternate way to add a new visit (only on iOS for now) is by tapping the 'add' button at the top left of the Visits tab which also has the option to select 'Other' to add a visit to a custom holy place not included.

Q: How do I record a visit from the past?

A: When you are recording the visit you can tap on the date to change it. It will default to today’s date so you’ll need to change it for past visits. For those you’ve already entered you can go into that entry from the visits tab and select edit to change date.

Q: How do I back-up my visits? Where is the data stored? Can it be synced to the cloud?

A: The data is stored in the app’s database on your device. On iOS, this data can be backed up to iCloud if your device is set up for nightly iCloud backups. On Android, backup behavior depends on your device and Google account settings. If you get a new device and restore it from the same backup, your visit history will appear on the new device. However, if the app is deleted or you set up a new device without restoring from backup, your visits will be lost.

To help prevent data loss, the app includes its own backup and restore feature, available from the Export button at the top right on the Visits tab. You can export your visit data as an XML file, which can later be used to restore your visits. When you choose to back up, you’ll be prompted to save the file to your preferred cloud storage service (e.g., iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or similar) or locally on your device. To restore from a backup, tap Import Visits from XML and select the previously exported XML file from your chosen storage location.

Q: How do I delete a visit?

A: On the Visits tab you can swipe left on a visit to reveal a Delete button. You can also delete all the visits entered at one time by deleting the app and reinstalling it.

Q: How do I see all the places I have visited on the map?

A: From the Map tab, you can click on the Filters button and filter the places to only those you have recorded visits to. This feature is only on iOS at this time.

Q: Is there a way to sync the app between devices?

A: The visits entered into the App are stored locally on the device and will not sync automatically between other devices. However, you can export the entered visits as a XML file and import them into a different device. To prevent duplicates, the App will only import visits that don't already exist on the device. The export/import options are available from the Visit tab’s Export button in the upper right corner. I recommend exporting the visits on a regular basis as a back-up since they would be lost if the app was mistakenly deleted.

Q: How do I track the time as a Temple Ordinance worker?

A: From the Settings page (accessible from the Home tab), you can turn on the 'Enable Hours Worked Entry' option. This will add an additional data entry element to the Record Visit page and also add a new reporting line to the Summary tab.

Q: Are there any plans to release the Holy Places app for the Android platform?

A: After years of anticipation, I am thrilled to announce that I have personally taken on the development of an Android version and it was released to the Play Store at the end of August 2025.
